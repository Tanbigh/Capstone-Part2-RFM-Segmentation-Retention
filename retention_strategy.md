# Retention Strategy

## Champions (440 Customers)

Characteristics:

* High recency score
* High purchase frequency
* High monetary value

Recommended Action:

* VIP rewards program
* Early access to new products
* Exclusive loyalty benefits

Business Value:
These customers generate significant revenue and should be retained at all costs.

---

## Loyal Customers (520 Customers)

Characteristics:

* Frequent purchasers
* Consistent engagement

Recommended Action:

* Loyalty points
* Personalized product recommendations
* Cross-selling campaigns

Business Value:
Strong repeat buyers with potential to become Champions.

---

## Potential Loyalists (847 Customers)

Characteristics:

* Moderate purchase history
* Growing engagement

Recommended Action:

* Personalized email campaigns
* Product bundles
* Limited-time incentives

Business Value:
Largest customer segment with high conversion potential.

---

## Dormant Customers (519 Customers)

Characteristics:

* Low recent activity
* Low purchase frequency

Recommended Action:

* Win-back campaigns
* Re-engagement emails
* Special comeback offers

Business Value:
Potential revenue recovery from inactive users.

---

## At Risk Customers (67 Customers)

Characteristics:

* Historically valuable customers
* Recent inactivity

Recommended Action:

* Priority retention outreach
* Personalized offers
* Customer success follow-up

Business Value:
Preventing churn in this segment provides immediate ROI.

---

## High Value Unhappy Customers (7 Customers)

Characteristics:

* High spending customers
* Multiple support issues

Recommended Action:

* Dedicated support resolution
* Escalated customer care
* Personal follow-up calls

Business Value:
Small segment but high revenue impact.

---

# Budget Prioritization

Given a limited retention budget, the recommended priority order is:

1. High Value Unhappy
2. At Risk
3. Champions
4. Loyal Customers
5. Potential Loyalists
6. Dormant

This prioritization balances revenue protection and customer lifetime value.
